Selected projects, from simulating light in tissue to building hardware that responds to the human body.

AI-Enhanced Temperature Mapping for Laser-Based Therapies

Final-year project, supervised by Professor Igor Meglinski, in collaboration with Aston’s ARCHA centre. A machine-learning pipeline that converts Monte Carlo laser fluence fields into spatiotemporal tissue temperature maps with uncertainty estimates, based on the Pennes bioheat equation.

Self-Stabilising Spoon for Parkinsonian Tremor

Project manager and electronics lead. Closed-loop control on an ESP32 with real-time IMU sensing, FreeRTOS firmware and a custom KiCad PCB. I also authored the technical file, including an ISO 14971 risk framework.

Reduced tremor by about 70% across a ±40° range.

EEG and SSVEP Signal Processing

A MATLAB pipeline from raw EEG to frequency analysis, using Butterworth filtering and FFT to isolate the brain’s response to flickering visual stimuli.

Vertical Jump Biomechanics

Vicon motion capture and AMTI force plates, benchmarking jump-height estimation methods against a Dempster gold-standard model.

Flight-time method accurate to an RMSE of about 1.7 cm.

Transtibial Prosthesis Design

Current group project. 2D sagittal gait analysis of Vicon and force-plate data in MATLAB, informing the design development of a prosthesis modelled in SolidWorks.
